# Eye Chart: product requirements

Eye Chart is a public web app at https://yazan-o.github.io/eye-chart/ that shows how well TestSprite's AI tester reads 24 real web widgets. No sign-in. All routes are hash routes.

## 1. Chart page (`#/`)
- One screen with no page scroll. Left: the lit 3D chart (`chart-canvas` inside `scene`), large. Right column: the robot wearing glasses, large, centered over the serif heading "Eye Chart" (`heading`, also the home link `wordmark`), with the round `agent-toggle` and the About icon `about-open` at the right; one line on what TestSprite is (`what-testsprite`); a one-line tagline (`tagline`); and under them the letter card.
- The chart key (`chart-key`) lies over the lit chart: 7 rows (`chart-row-1` to `chart-row-7`, their Snellen labels 20/200 down to 20/25 present for assistive readers only). Each of the 24 letters is a link `letter-link-<id>` placed on its letter; its accessible name and its hover label give the letter name and its state (Read correctly, Misread, Blocked, Not read yet). Row 1 holds one letter (Counter); row 7 holds the hidden-text letters.
- The letter card (`letter-card`, with `data-letter-id`) is always open: at load on the first misread letter with a recorded lens fix, and on whichever letter was clicked last on the chart. It shows `card-name`, the trap in one line, `card-state` (Read correctly, Misread, Blocked, Not read yet, or "Fixed with lens" on the last step of a fixed letter), step tabs (`card-tab-works`, `card-tab-broken`, `card-tab-verdict`, and for misread or blocked letters with a lens `card-tab-lens`, `card-tab-after`), the live widget on the works and broken steps with `card-line` naming what it does ("This version works. …", "This version is broken. …"), then the verdict step: an answer key `card-key` with one row per twin ("Working version, should pass, TestSprite said passed, ✓ right" / "Broken version, should fail, TestSprite said passed, ✗ missed the bug"), the bold line `card-verdict` ("TestSprite read this letter wrong." / "right." / "could not test this letter." / "has not read this letter yet."), and `card-line` "Every letter on the chart is scored this way."; at that moment the same mark (slash, bar or hatch) lands again on that letter in the 3D chart. A letter read right ends there. A misread or blocked letter with a lens continues: the lens step (`card-lens`, the small real code change, `card-line` "Can we help it see? Yes: a lens, a tiny code change that lets the AI see the widget…") and the result step (`card-key` again with the lens readings, `card-verdict` "Fixed with the lens." or "Still wrong with the lens.", and the chart letter's mark turns into a tick when fixed). `data-step` names the step (`works`, `broken`, `verdict`, `lens`, `after`). `card-back` and `card-next` step, the tabs jump, left and right arrows and a swipe step too, and `card-details` opens the station at `#/letter/<id>`.
- After the chart lands, once per visit, the card advances by itself (`data-playing="true"`) about every five seconds through its steps; any click or key stops the advance.
- `about-open` (top right icon) opens the About panel; it can be closed again (`about-close`, Escape or the scrim). The panel holds: the tally (`tally`) with four counts and a one-line meaning each: read correctly (`tally-read`), misread (`tally-misread`), blocked (`tally-blocked`), not read yet (`tally-unread`), always adding up to 24; `reading-summary` ("Read correctly: N of 24 letters" and the measurement date, N equal to `tally-read`); `twin-summary` ("Twin readings correct: N of M"); `plaque` (the recorded run and its commit); the lens machine (section 3); `motion-toggle`, which pauses and resumes motion (its label changes to say motion is paused, and pressing it again restores "Pause motion"); `about-totals` ("The readings file holds N readings: M twin readings, K of them correct, and L lens readings."), `about-letters` (the letter counts by state, matching `tally-*` and `twin-summary` with no lens on); and `github-link`, which opens the source on GitHub.

## 2. TestSprite view (`agent-toggle`)
- The round mark top right (TestSprite's own logo in a circle with "Test" over "Sprite" under it; text "TestSprite view", "Human view" while on; aria-label matches) switches the page into the sensor view: a slim strip under the chart (`sensor-count`, never covering the chart or the card) reports how many parts of the page the agent can find and how many canvases it cannot see into; the live status line `agent-summary` starts "Sensor view.".
- Pressing the toggle again, `agent-exit` or Escape returns to the normal view and the strip goes away.

## 3. Lens machine (`lens-machine`)
- Inside the About panel, lists the lenses with a dial each (`lens-dial-<id>`), all "Off" at load. While the card shows a letter's lens, that letter's dial lights up but stays "Off" and unpressed.
- Turning a dial on switches that letter's chart state to the reading taken with the lens; turning it off restores the original state. The letter's link `letter-link-<id>` carries `data-lens="on"` or `data-lens="off"` (letters without a lens carry no `data-lens`).

## 4. Letter station (`#/letter/<id>`)
- Shows the letter's name, row and state, the trap in one sentence, and the working and broken versions side by side with an "Open alone" link each.
- Shows the truth for each version, TestSprite's verdict and its stated reason. When TestSprite left a screenshot, `reading-<twin>-screenshot` opens it larger (`reading-<twin>-screenshot-zoom`); Escape or Close shuts it.
- A switch (`pane-switch`) has buttons `pane-works`, `pane-broken` and, for letters with a lens, `pane-lens`, each with `aria-pressed`. "Lens" puts the lens twin (`station-lens-twin`) in place of the twin it fixes, the big letter (`station-glyph`) fuses its ghost and shows a tick when the recorded lens reading is correct, and `station-state` reads "Fixed with lens." with `data-lens="on"`. At 375 px one twin shows at a time.
- Letters with a lens show the lens name, summary and an exploded view (`lens-exploded`): the twin as TestSprite read it (`lens-before`), the lens's real code change (`lens-diff`), and the lens twin's reading (`reading-lens`), joined as "twin + lens = lens twin". `lens-explode` assembles and separates the parts.
- Previous and next links move between letters.

## 5. Twin pages (`#/l/<id>/works`, `#/l/<id>/broken`)
- Each renders one widget alone, with no chart around it.
- Example: `#/l/counter/works` shows a counter; clicking its button three times shows "Count: 3". `#/l/counter/broken` shows the wrong count after three clicks.

## 6. General
- Unknown routes show a not-found page with a link back to the chart.
- The page works at phone width (375 px) with no horizontal scrolling.
- With reduced motion requested, the chart does not animate.
